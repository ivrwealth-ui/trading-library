# Appendix — Strategy Quick-Reference Table

This table is a summary, not a substitute for the full chapters. Adjust thresholds to your own risk tolerance, universe, and data sources.

| # | Idea | Fundamental screen (what) | Technical trigger (when) | Exit |
|---|------|---------------------------|--------------------------|------|
| 1 | Quality + Uptrend | Sustained ROE/ROCE > 15%, low debt | Pullback reversal or breakout above rising 200-day MA | Below 200-day MA / stop |
| 2 | Growth + Breakout | Revenue/EPS growth > 15–20%, stable margins | Volume-confirmed breakout to a new high | Trailing stop (20/50-day MA) |
| 3 | Value + Reversal | Low P/E/P/B (below own median), viable, profitable | Higher low + close above 50-day MA on volume | Below reversal low / trend break |

| 5 | Earnings Momentum + RS | Accelerating EPS, rising estimates, revenue confirmation | Outperformance (RS) + breakout/pullback in uptrend | Negative surprise / RS breakdown |

## The two questions every idea answers

1. **What** — is this a good business? (the fundamental screen)
2. **When** — is the market rewarding it now? (the technical trigger)

## The universal rules

- Fundamentals decide the universe; technicals decide the timing.
- Write the screen and the exits down, before any trade.
- Set the stop, trend exit, and thesis exit at entry.
- Re-screen on a schedule (monthly/quarterly), not on headlines.
- Own fewer things, less often — the discipline *is* the edge.
