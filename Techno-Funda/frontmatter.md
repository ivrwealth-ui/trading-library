## How to Read This Book

This book teaches **techno-funda** investing — the deliberate fusion of *fundamental* analysis (what to buy: quality, growth, value) with *technical* analysis (when to buy and when to exit). It presents five strategy ideas, each pairing a fundamental screen with a technical trigger.

- **Chapters 1–2** explain what techno-funda is and why the combination works.
- **Chapters 3–4** lay out the two halves: the fundamental screen and the technical timing layer.
- **Chapters 5–9** are the five strategy ideas — the heart of the book. Each is self-contained: the idea, the fundamental criteria, the technical trigger, an Indian-market example, and its risks.
- **Chapter 10** turns the ideas into a repeatable screening workflow.
- The **appendix** condenses all five ideas into a quick-reference table.

A note on language: this book describes what a strategy *is* and what conditions it historically aligns with — never what you "should" do. Where results are discussed, they are framed as historical observations, not promises. You are responsible for your own decisions.
