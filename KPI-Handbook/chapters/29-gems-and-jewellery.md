# 29. Gems and Jewellery

**At a glance.** Two businesses: jewellery *retail* (organized players selling gold/diamond jewellery to Indian consumers — a brand + trust + inventory game) and diamond *processing/export* (India cuts & polishes most of the world's diamonds). Gold prices drive the first; rough and polished diamond prices and US/China demand drive the second. Listed leaders: retail — Titan (Tanishq), Kalyan Jewellers, Senco Gold; processing — (mostly integrated or private, with few pure listed plays).

## The KPIs — Jewellery Retail

**Same-store sales growth (SSSG)** — revenue growth from existing stores.

*Why it matters:* Jewellery is a store-based retail business (see the Retail chapter); SSSG strips out store additions to show true underlying demand.

*How to read:* Watch SSSG against gold-price moves (gold-price inflation flatters revenue). Real SSSG (volume/value adjusted for gold price) is the honest signal.

*Source:* Quarterly results (Titan and Kalyan disclose SSSG).

**Studded (diamond) vs plain gold mix** — the product split.

*Why it matters:* Studded/diamond jewellery carries higher margins than plain gold. The mix is the margin lever.

*How to read:* A rising studded share is margin-positive. Watch the wedding-driven mix seasonality.

*Source:* Quarterly results and annual report.

**Gold price and inventory (gold on lease)** — the raw-material exposure.

*Why it matters:* Jewellers hold large gold inventory, often funded by gold leases (borrowed gold). Gold-price swings create inventory gains/losses, and lease costs are the funding expense.

*How to read:* Strip gold inventory gains/losses to see the operating margin. Watch the gold-on-lease position (hedged vs unhedged) for price risk.

*Source:* Quarterly results and MD&A.

**Store count and additions** — the retail footprint.

*Why it matters:* Organized jewellery retail is gaining share from unorganized players; store expansion is the growth engine.

*How to read:* Watch store additions and whether SSSG stays positive (healthy expansion) or turns negative (over-expansion).

*Source:* Quarterly results.

## The KPIs — Diamond Processing

**Rough diamond prices vs polished prices** — the input and output prices.

*Why it matters:* The processing margin is the spread between rough (input) and polished (output) prices, both global. A squeeze (rough up, polished down) crushes margins.

*How to read:* Watch the rough/polished spread and global demand (US and China are the buyers). Lab-grown diamonds (LGD) are a structural disruptor to monitor.

*Source:* Industry data (Rapaport and rough-diamond price indexes) and management commentary.

**Export demand and customer mix** — the global order book.

*Why it matters:* India's diamond industry is export-led; demand tracks US/China consumer spending.

*How to read:* Watch export volumes and the customer mix; concentration in one market is a risk.

*Source:* GJEPC (Gem & Jewellery Export Promotion Council) data.

## Healthy profile vs red flags

- **Healthy:** Positive real SSSG, rising studded share, well-hedged gold inventory, disciplined store expansion.
- **Red flags:** Revenue growth flattered only by gold-price inflation, falling studded share, unhedged gold inventory in a volatile market, or negative SSSG on store expansion.
