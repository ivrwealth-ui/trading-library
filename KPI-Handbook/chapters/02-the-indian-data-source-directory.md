# 2. The Indian Data-Source Directory

Most operational KPIs are disclosed by companies themselves in their **quarterly investor presentations** (the fastest place) and **annual report MD&A** (the detailed place). They are then re-published and made comparable by the free tools below. "Primary" sources are the authoritative origin of a number; "live tools" are the fastest way to read, screen and compare it.

> **A caution on third-party tools.** Screeners and aggregators re-publish data that companies filed elsewhere. They can lag, mis-tag a figure, or apply their own definition of a ratio. Where a number matters, trace it back to the company's filing on BSE/NSE. Several of these sites also publish ratings, scores or buy/sell-style calls; this book lists them as **data sources only** and does not endorse those opinions.

## 2.6 A note on annual reports

The ultimate primary source for any KPI is the **annual report** — available from the company's investor page and BSE/NSE filings. Read three sections closely:

1. **Management Discussion & Analysis (MD&A)** — where management explains the *drivers* behind the operational KPIs (volume, price, mix) and flags risks.
2. **Notes to accounts** — where accounting choices are disclosed and where KPI definitions can subtly change year to year.
3. **Related-party transactions and contingent liabilities** — where hidden obligations often live.
