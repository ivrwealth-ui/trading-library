# 8. Pharmaceuticals and Biotech

**At a glance.** Indian pharma splits into two very different businesses: the **domestic branded business** (chronic/acute therapies sold to Indian doctors) and the **export business** (US generics, other regulated markets, and emerging markets). The US generics business is driven by filings, approvals and price erosion; the domestic business by therapy mix and field-force reach. Listed leaders include Sun Pharma, Cipla, Dr Reddy's, Divi's Labs, and Lupin.

## The KPIs

**Revenue mix — domestic vs US vs other exports** — the geographic split.

*Why it matters:* Each geography has a different profitability and risk profile. US generics are high-revenue but face relentless price erosion; domestic branded is steadier and higher-margin.

*How to read:* Watch the *shift* in mix. A rising domestic share is generally re-rated positively; a rising US share must be read with price-erosion and approval momentum.

*Source:* Quarterly investor presentation.

**US price erosion** — the year-on-year decline in US generic prices (a sector-level number).

*Why it matters:* US generic pricing is structurally deflationary due to buyer consolidation (the big three wholesalers). Every US-exposed firm fights this tide.

*How to read:* Erosion of 5–10% a year is the norm; firms beat it by launching *new* products faster than old ones deflate. A firm growing US revenue *despite* erosion is gaining share.

*Source:* Management commentary on earnings calls; sector reports.

**ANDA / DMF filings and approvals** — Abbreviated New Drug Applications are filed with, and approved by, the US FDA; Drug Master Files (confidential manufacturing dossiers) are filed but not "approved".

*Why it matters:* These are the pipeline. Approvals today are revenue tomorrow; filings today are revenue two years out.

*How to read:* Track the cumulative pending ANDA count and the annual approval run-rate. A firm with a thin pipeline faces a revenue cliff as existing products erode.

*Source:* Quarterly presentation and FDA databases.

**USFDA compliance status — inspections, 483s, Warning Letters** — the regulatory health of a company's plants.

*Why it matters:* A Warning Letter or import alert on a key plant freezes US approvals and can halt shipments — a direct hit to the export business. It is the single biggest binary risk in pharma.

*How to read:* Clean/positive inspection outcomes are the baseline; track the number of facilities under warning letters. Resolution (lifting of a warning letter) is a major positive catalyst.

*Source:* US FDA website and company disclosures.

**R&D spend as % of revenue** — investment in new molecules, complex generics and biosimilars.

*Why it matters:* R&D intensity signals the firm's bet on the future — complex generics and specialty products are the defence against commodity price erosion.

*How to read:* 6–12% of sales is typical for serious players; heavy R&D without a corresponding product pipeline is a cost drag, not a moat.

*Source:* Annual report.

**Domestic chronic vs acute mix** — the share of revenue from chronic therapies (diabetes, cardiac, CNS) versus acute (anti-infectives, pain).

*Why it matters:* Chronic prescriptions are stickier and more resilient; acute is more price- and season-sensitive. Chronic-heavy portfolios command higher multiples.

*How to read:* A rising chronic share is a quality upgrade. Compare against the Indian market's roughly 35–40% chronic share (industry estimates vary by source).

*Source:* Quarterly presentation and annual report (therapy-wise break-up).

**Gross margin and EBITDA margin** — profitability (higher than most industries; pharma gross margins often exceed 60%).

*Why it matters:* Margins reveal pricing power and manufacturing efficiency.

*How to read:* Gross margins of 55–75% and EBITDA margins of 18–30% are common. A falling gross margin usually signals raw-material (API) cost pressure or US price erosion.

*Source:* **Screener.in** and quarterly results.

## Healthy profile vs red flags

- **Healthy:** Balanced mix, rising chronic share, growing ANDA pipeline, clean FDA status, R&D 6%+.
- **Red flags:** Revenue concentrated in a few US products facing patent expiry, a Warning Letter on a major plant, falling gross margin, or R&D spending with no pipeline to show for it.
