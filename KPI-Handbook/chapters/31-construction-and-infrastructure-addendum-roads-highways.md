# 31. Construction & Infrastructure — Addendum (Roads/Highways)

**At a glance.** This brief addendum sits alongside the EPC chapter for the asset-heavy road developers that build highways under government concessions. The road-asset model (BOT-toll and HAM) has its own KPIs. Listed leaders include IRB Infrastructure (BOT/HAM), and diversified infra players with road portfolios.

## The KPIs

**Toll revenue growth and traffic growth** — revenue from toll collection and vehicle traffic.

*Why it matters:* For a BOT-toll asset, toll revenue is the annuity, and traffic is the underlying driver. Traffic growth tracks economic activity and the corridor's maturity.

*How to read:* Watch traffic (PCUs — passenger car units) growth and toll-rate escalations (linked to inflation). A mature asset with growing traffic and indexed tolls is a compounding annuity.

*Source:* Quarterly results and NHAI data.

**HAM annuity inflows and equity commitment** — the annuity receipts and the equity the developer must fund.

*Why it matters:* Under HAM, the government (NHAI) funds roughly 40% of the project cost during construction and repays the rest, with interest and O&M, as fixed annuities; the developer funds the balance through equity and debt. The annuity is contracted revenue; the developer's equity commitment is the cash burden.

*How to read:* Watch the annuity stream build up and the equity-funding requirement. Over-committing equity to many HAM projects strains the balance sheet.

*Source:* Quarterly results and annual report.

**Asset monetization (InvITs / portfolio sales)** — selling completed road assets to infrastructure investment trusts or buyers.

*Why it matters:* Monetization releases trapped capital, de-levers the balance sheet and is the source of value realization for asset-heavy developers.

*How to read:* Watch monetization events and proceeds — a developer that can build, de-lever and recycle capital creates the most value.

*Source:* Corporate announcements.

## Healthy profile vs red flags

- **Healthy:** Growing toll traffic, indexed tolls, contracted HAM annuity stream, disciplined monetization.
- **Red flags:** Over-committed equity across many projects, stalled land/acquisition delays, or dependence on refinancing to service debt.
