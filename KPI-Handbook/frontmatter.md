## How This Book Is Organised

- **Part I — Foundations** (Chapters 1–2): how to use the book, and the directory of where to source Indian market data.
- **Part II — Industry Chapters** (Chapters 3–31): 29 industry chapters, each with the business in brief, the KPIs that matter, and a healthy-profile-vs-red-flags checklist. **Chapter 32** is a one-page primer on reading any operational KPI.
- **Part III — Quick Reference** (appendix): a master table of the top KPIs per industry, and a glossary.
