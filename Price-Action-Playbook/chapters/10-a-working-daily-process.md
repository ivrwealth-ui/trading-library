# 10. A Working Daily Process

Price action is a skill, and skills are built with a repeatable process. Here is a complete loop for the Indian session.

## Before the open (08:45–09:10)

1. **Mark the context.** On the daily and hourly, name the structure: trend or range. Note the daily trend's direction and its most recent swing level.
2. **Mark the levels.** Plot: previous day high/low/close, the prior swing highs/lows, round numbers, and any obvious volume-at-price POC.
3. **Plan, don't predict.** Write down the two or three scenarios and the level that *invalidates* each. Decide in advance where the market would have to go for your read to be wrong.

## During the session

1. **Read the opening range** (09:15–09:30). Note whether price accepts or rejects it.
2. **Trade only the setups in this playbook** — failed breakouts at levels, break-and-retests in a trend, inside-bar compressions at value — and only in the auction hours.
3. **Let the higher timeframe lead.** If the daily is an uptrend, lean long; short setups (failed rallies) are lower conviction.

## After the close (15:35)

1. **Journal every decision**, not just every trade. Record what you saw, what you did, and what actually happened.
2. **Review the failed setups too** — the trades you *didn't* take teach as much as the ones you did.
3. **Update tomorrow's levels** from today's session (today's high/low/close and POC become tomorrow's references).
