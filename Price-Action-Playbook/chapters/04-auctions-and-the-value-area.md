# 4. Auctions and the Value Area

Auction Market Theory (AMT) describes price as an auction whose job is to **discover value** and **facilitate trade**. The market's goal is not to "go up" or "go down"; it is to find a price where buyers and sellers can do the most business.

## The core concepts

- **Value** — the price area where the most trade occurs; where both sides agree.
- **The Point of Control (POC)** — the single price with the greatest traded volume; the auction's centre of gravity.
- **The Value Area** — the range (usually around 70%) where most of a period's volume occurred.
- **Acceptance** — price spending time inside value; the market is comfortable there.
- **Rejection** — price leaving value quickly; the market is signalling that value has changed.

## The market profile / TPO view

In the West, this is visualised with a **market profile** (a horizontal histogram of time-at-price, with lettered time-price-opportunity rows). In India, the concepts are applied with the same tools many brokers and platforms provide, or approximated from volume-at-price and the opening range.

**How to read the profile:**

- A **single, well-defined value area** = a balanced, rotational market. Expect range behaviour and mean-reversion, not trend.
- A **wide, poorly defined value area** = disagreement and transition.
- A **shift in value** — the POC and value area migrating higher or lower day over day — is the profile's own statement of a trend, and often a more honest one than any indicator.

## Why value matters to a price-action trader

Value is where **the other side of your trade lives**. Breakouts work because price leaves value and forces participants to re-evaluate; breakouts fail when price leaves value but no one follows, and it snaps back to value. Understanding where value is tells you, in advance, where the two-sided auction is likely to resolve.

**Practical proxies for value on an Indian chart:**

- The **opening range** and the **initial balance** (the first 15 minutes and the first hour).
- The **previous day's high, low and close** (prior value references).
- **Volume-at-price** clusters (where the most contracts changed hands).
- The **developing POC** of the current session.
