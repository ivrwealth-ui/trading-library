# 2. Market Structure

Market structure is the skeleton of price action. Every chart, at every timeframe, is made of the same basic material: **swing highs**, **swing lows**, and the sequence they form.

## The building blocks

- **Swing high** — a price bar whose high is higher than the bars on both sides.
- **Swing low** — a price bar whose low is lower than the bars on both sides.
- **Higher High (HH) / Higher Low (HL)** — rising tops and rising bottoms.
- **Lower High (LH) / Lower Low (LL)** — falling tops and falling bottoms.

**How to read:** An **uptrend** is a sequence of HH and HL — each pullback finds support *above* the previous low. A **downtrend** is LH and LL — each rally stalls *below* the previous high. A **range** is neither — price oscillates between two boundaries without making progress. Most of the work in price action is simply naming which of the three the market is in, and on which timeframe.

## Structure breaks and "market shifts"

The single most important event in price action is a **structure break** — the first lower low in an uptrend, or the first higher high in a downtrend. It is the earliest objective evidence that the balance of power is changing.

**How to read a structure break:**

- A break of the **most recent swing low** in an uptrend is the first warning. It does not mean the trend is over; it means the trend is now in question.
- A **change of character** (often abbreviated CHoCH) is when price not only breaks a swing but then *fails to make a new extreme* and instead starts carving the opposite structure — LH/LL after an uptrend, or HH/HL after a downtrend. That is the higher-confidence signal.
- The opposite — and the most dangerous event for a trader — is the **breakout failure** — see Chapter 7.

**The trend is a hierarchy, not a fact.** Price can be in an uptrend on the daily, a range on the hourly, and a downtrend on the 5-minute — all at once. Before you act, decide which timeframe is your *decision* timeframe and which are your *context* timeframes. A classic mistake is to trade a 5-minute pullback against the daily trend and wonder why the move failed.

## Structure checklist

- Identify the last **two** swing highs and **two** swing lows on your decision timeframe.
- Name the structure: uptrend (HH/HL), downtrend (LH/LL), or range.
- Mark the most recent swing low (in an uptrend) or swing high (in a downtrend) — that is the level whose break changes the story.
