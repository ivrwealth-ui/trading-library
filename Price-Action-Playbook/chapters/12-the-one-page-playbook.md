# 12. The One-Page Playbook

The entire book, compressed to a single page you can keep in front of you.

**Read the market (before anything):**

- Name the structure on the daily and hourly: uptrend, downtrend, or range.
- Locate value: opening range, previous day high/low, volume POC.

**Trade these setups, in this order of preference:**

1. **Failed breakout** at a clear level (spring or upthrust).
2. **Break-and-retest** of a broken level, with the higher-timeframe trend.
3. **Inside-bar compression** breakout at a level or value.
4. **Pullback** to value in an established trend.
5. **Reversal** only with structure-break evidence at a confluence of levels.

**Every trade needs:**

- A level that defines the idea.
- A stop beyond the level (where the idea is wrong).
- An R-multiple target that justifies the risk.
- A one-sentence reason and a one-sentence invalidation.

**Context rules that override everything:**

- Trade with the daily trend when you can; counter-trend only on failed breakouts.
- Be aggressive in the open and close auctions; patient in the midday lull.
- Two losses in a row → stop and reassess.
- No level, no trade. No invalidation, no trade.
