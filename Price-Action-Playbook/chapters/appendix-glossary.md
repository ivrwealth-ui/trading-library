# Glossary

**Acceptance** — price spending time in an area; the market is comfortable with value there.

**Balance** — a rotational, two-sided market (a range).

**Break-and-retest** — a level breaks, price continues, then returns to retest it from the other side.

**Change of character (CHoCH)** — the first opposite swing that suggests a trend is turning.

**Confluence** — multiple levels/references coinciding at one price, strengthening it.

**Failed breakout** — a break of a level that fails to hold and reverses back through it.

**HH / HL / LH / LL** — higher high / higher low / lower high / lower low (structure points).

**Imbalance** — a directional, one-sided market (a trend).

**Inside bar** — a bar whose range is wholly contained within the previous bar.

**Initial balance** — the range of the first hour of the session (09:15–10:15 IST).

**Opening range** — the first 15 minutes of the session (09:15–09:30 IST); the day's first statement of value.

**POC (Point of Control)** — the price with the greatest traded volume.

**Rejection** — price leaving an area quickly, signalling that value has changed.

**Spring** — a failed breakdown below support that reverses back above it.

**Upthrust** — a failed breakout above resistance that reverses back below it.

**Value area** — the price range (≈70%) where most of a period's volume occurred.
