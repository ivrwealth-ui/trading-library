## The common machinery

Every strategy reduces to four settings:

1. **Universe** — what you rank.
2. **Lookback** — over what window (6–12 months, skip the last month).
3. **Selection** — top decile, top N, or above a threshold.
4. **Rebalance** — the schedule that enforces "sell weakness, buy strength."

## The universal rules

- **Combine relative + absolute momentum** wherever possible (relative picks *what*, absolute decides *whether*).
- **Add a broad 200-day filter** to avoid bear markets.
- **Minimise turnover** — the lower-cost, lower-tax variant usually wins net.
- **Run momentum as a satellite** around a broad core, not the whole portfolio.
- **Judge over 5–10 years, not quarters** — the premium is lumpy by design.
