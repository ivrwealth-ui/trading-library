## How to Read This Book

This book teaches **momentum investing** — the long-horizon, factor-based approach to capturing the momentum premium — through five historically documented strategies. It is the patient cousin of momentum *trading*: lower turnover, longer holding periods, and a focus on *systematic rules* over market timing.

- **Chapters 1–3** build the foundation: what momentum investing is, the evidence behind the momentum factor, and the toolkit (trailing returns, lookbacks, rebalancing, absolute vs. relative momentum).
- **Chapters 4–8** are the five strategies — the heart of the book. Each is self-contained and presented with its logic, rules, historical context, an Indian-market example, and its risks.
- **Chapters 9–10** cover portfolio construction, costs, taxes, risk, and the psychology of staying the course over years.
- The **appendix** condenses all five strategies into a quick-reference table.

A note on language: this book describes what a strategy *is* and what it has *historically* tended to do — never what you "should" do. Where results are discussed, they are framed as historical observations, not promises. You are responsible for your own decisions.
