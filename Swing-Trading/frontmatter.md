## How to Read This Book

This book teaches **swing trading** — capturing multi-day price moves over a holding period of a few days to a few weeks — through five historically grounded strategies. Swing trading sits between day trading (too fast) and investing (too slow), and it draws on *both* of the two great market tendencies: **momentum** (trends continue) and **mean reversion** (extremes snap back).

- **Chapters 1–3** build the foundation: what swing trading is, the toolkit (support/resistance, moving averages, RSI, volume, trend structure), and how to read whether a market is trending or ranging.
- **Chapters 4–8** are the five strategies — the heart of the book. Three are *continuation* (trend-following) and two are *reversion* (fade-the-extreme). Each is self-contained, with rules, historical context, an Indian-market example, and its risks.
- **Chapters 9–10** cover trade plans, risk management, and the psychology of holding multi-day positions.
- The **appendix** condenses all five strategies into a quick-reference table.

A note on language: this book describes what a strategy *is* and what conditions it historically aligns with — never what you "should" do. Where results are discussed, they are framed as historical observations, not promises. You are responsible for your own decisions.
