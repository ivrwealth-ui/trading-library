## How to Read This Book

This book teaches **momentum trading** — the discipline of buying strength and riding trends — through five historically documented strategies. Each strategy is presented the same way: the idea behind it, the exact rules, a worked Indian-market example, and its risks and limitations.

- **Chapters 1–3** build the foundation: what momentum trading is, why it works, and the toolkit (indicators and concepts) the strategies use.
- **Chapters 4–8** are the five strategies — the heart of the book. Read them as a menu, not a sequence: each is self-contained.
- **Chapters 9–10** cover the two things that matter more than any strategy: risk management and psychology.
- The **appendix** condenses all five strategies into a quick-reference table.

A note on language: this book describes what a strategy *is* and what conditions it historically aligns with — never what you "should" do. Where results are discussed, they are framed as historical observations ("historically, conditions like this have favoured…"), not promises. You are responsible for your own decisions.
