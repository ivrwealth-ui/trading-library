## The common thread

Every strategy reduces to the same three steps:

1. **Define strength** with a rule.
2. **Enter on strength**, not hunches.
3. **Exit on weakness**, with a rule — never on emotion.

## The universal risk rules

- Risk **1–2%** of capital per trade.
- Size the position from the **stop distance**, not from conviction.
- Cut losses short; let winners run.
- Add a broad-market (200-day MA) filter to avoid bear markets.
- Diversify; watch net exposure across the whole book.
